## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm **Mojib Rsm**, also known as Mojibur Rahman, a Bangladeshi digital entrepreneur, technology professional, and digital creator passionate about building practical digital products and technology-driven solutions.

My work spans web development, digital entrepreneurship, website management, search engine optimization (SEO), digital publishing, online tools, automation, and creative digital projects.

I enjoy transforming ideas into useful online platforms, improving digital experiences, and developing solutions that address real-world needs.

- 🚀 **Entrepreneurship:** Building digital brands, online platforms, and technology-based services.
- 🌐 **Web Development:** Developing websites, web applications, and online service platforms.
- 📈 **SEO & Growth:** Exploring technical SEO, website performance, and organic search visibility.
- 📰 **Digital Publishing:** Working with information platforms, online content, and digital resources.
- 🧰 **Automation:** Developing practical tools, bots, APIs, and workflow solutions.
- 🎨 **Creative Technology:** Interested in UI design, digital branding, content creation, and visual communication.
- 🤝 **Leadership & Community:** Interested in community initiatives, collaboration, and technology-driven development.
- 🇧🇩 **Based in:** Ramu, Cox's Bazar, Bangladesh.

**My vision:** Build useful digital products, strengthen online experiences, and create long-term value through technology.
