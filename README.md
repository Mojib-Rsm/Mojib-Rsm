## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm **Mojib Rsm**, also known formally as Mojibur Rahman, a Bangladeshi digital entrepreneur and technology professional passionate about building useful digital products, web platforms, online services, and technology-driven solutions.

My work combines technology, entrepreneurship, digital publishing, search engine optimization, and practical problem-solving. I enjoy turning ideas into functional digital experiences that help people access information, services, and online resources more easily.

- 🚀 **Digital Entrepreneurship:** Building and developing digital brands and online platforms.
- 🌐 **Web Development:** Creating websites, web applications, and online service platforms.
- 📈 **SEO & Digital Growth:** Working on technical SEO, content optimization, and organic visibility.
- 📰 **Digital Publishing:** Exploring online news, knowledge resources, and content-driven platforms.
- 🧰 **Automation & Tools:** Developing practical utilities, bots, and workflow solutions.
- 🎨 **Digital Creativity:** Interested in UI design, branding, content creation, and digital media.
- 🇧🇩 **Location:** Ramu, Cox's Bazar, Bangladesh.

My goal is to build sustainable digital products that solve real-world problems and create long-term value.
